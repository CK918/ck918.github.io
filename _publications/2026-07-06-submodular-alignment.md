Can safety alignment be made robust during training using the smallest amount of data possible? We frame data augmentation for safety alignment as a submodular optimization problem, treating the selection of safety data as a submodular cover problem. The resulting framework provably hardens a model's safety, achieving near-zero attack success rates with zero inference-time overhead.
